# scenes/bodies

What: the size and sockets of every creature and wizard (`BodyFrame` scenes in `units/bodies/` and `wizards/bodies/`: capsule, footprint, reach, center, top, muzzle, feet; `BodyFacts` reads them once, `BodyMount` says which one a body wears; its `Look` is a placeholder of `<name>_look.tscn`, the `BodyLook` with the model, the sounds and the carried effect, which only a client builds) and the 74 skeletal models (`models/<name>_model.tscn`, named after the creature or wizard that wears it; the tag that travels on the wire, `eorc`, stays the `model` of the scene and `ModelFiles` in `models/model_files.tres` maps it to the file: `BodyModel.scene_path(tag)`) and what draws them: each has a library of clips by role (`<tag>_clips.tres`, one Animation per role with its exact length, `hit`/`shoot` markers and event calls) and a Tree with one state per role; `BodyRig` plays a role, `ClipFacts` gives the rules the same numbers. Each model can also carry a physical skeleton (`Skeleton3D/Ragdoll`, baked by `tools/bake/bake_ragdolls.gd`) that its body falls or hangs with.
Not: what a creature does (that is `units/`) or how long a swing lasts in the match (the rules read `ClipFacts`, they never play a clip).
Talks to: a body calls `BodyMount.wear(type.body)`; `BodyCollider` sizes the physics capsule from `BodyMount.facts`; melee, avoidance and formations read `BodyFacts.of_body(body)`. on a client `BodyMount` hands the look to `BodyRig.mount`, `BodyAnimator` listens to it and asks `BodyRig` for a role; `HandFx` reads the hand sockets (`HandL`/`HandR` under the skeleton of the model, shown by a `visible` track of the clip, hidden by `RESET`); `UnitSounds`, `WizardSounds`, `CommandVoice` and `CreatureFx` read `BodyMount.look`; `BodyRig` travels the model's Tree; the Animations call `ClipEvents.fire(kind)`; `UnitBody`/`WizardBody` read `ClipFacts.of(library)`; `RagdollDriver` (one per body, pose `OFF`/`FALL`/`HANG`, asked by `CorpseLook`, `WizardDeathLook` and later `CaptiveLook`) binds the model the rig mounts (`bind_model`, signal `bound`) and reads `BodyModel.ragdoll` and `BodyModel.hang_bone`; `RagdollPose` holds its math; `BodyVisual.fade_to` is the only owner of the body's fade tween.
Edit: open `scenes/units/bodies/<name>.tscn` (or `scenes/wizards/bodies/`) and drag `Bounds/Capsule`, `Bounds/Footprint` and the `Sockets/` markers · open `models/<tag>.tscn` (F6): `Clips` > Animation panel for a role (length, `hit` marker, method calls), `Tree` for the states; a new model is `tools/bake/new_creature.gd` · the ragdoll of a model: `models/<tag>.tscn` > `Skeleton3D/Ragdoll` (bones, joints) and the `Ragdoll` group of the root (`ragdoll`, `hang_bone`: the bone a hanging body dangles from, a child of `ragdoll`; empty = it never hangs) · fall and hang knobs (`settle_s`, `bone_damping`, `hang_damping`) on the `RagdollDriver` node of the body.
Check: `debug/suite/presentation_refs_cases.gd`, `debug/suite/body_facts_cases.gd` and `debug/suite/clip_facts_cases.gd` and `debug/suite/death_cases.gd` (ragdoll falls and stands up; rules_suite) · `debug/bodies/body_gallery.tscn` (F6: every body with its look) · `debug/bodies/unit_parity_gallery.tscn`.

---

Cuerpos y modelos: animador, cajas, tamanos (`BodyFacts`) y `models/`. Los `.tscn` de modelos se editan en el editor (se llaman `<criatura>_model.tscn`; el codigo original, p. ej. `eorc`, sigue siendo el `model` de la escena y el id del cable, y `models/model_files.tres` dice que archivo es); los clips de cada uno viven en `models/<tag>_clips.tres` (la biblioteca) y `models/<tag>_clips/<clip>.res` (un archivo por clip, con sus pistas de huesos, el marcador del golpe y los eventos). Un modelo nuevo sale de `tools/bake/new_creature.gd`.

Agregar uno nuevo: poner el script y la escena aca, componer con nodos hijos y `@export` (nada de herencia profunda), y los datos de tipo en `data/` como `.tres`. Las pruebas de este dominio van en `debug/bodies/`.

Un modelo propio (RFC-051): un `.glb` en `assets/custom/models/` y `tools/bake/new_creature.gd` (o Project > Tools > Sacrifice: Nueva criatura desde modelo…) escriben su `models/<tag>.tscn`, su biblioteca de clips, su escena de cuerpo, su apariencia (`<tag>_look.tscn`) y su `CreatureType`; ver «Crear una criatura propia desde Blender» en `docs/HOWTO.md`. Si reexportas el `.glb`, `new_creature.gd --sync` copia las pistas de huesos de nuevo a las Animations y conserva los marcadores.

`IdleSway` (idle_sway.gd): hijo opcional de un cuerpo que respira mientras esta en idle (sube y baja unos centimetros y se inclina un par de grados) moviendo solo el `pivot` que dibuja (el BodyRig), nunca el cuerpo. Es lo que anima al Sac Doctor, cuyo idle es una pose quieta (cuadro 10 de SDst, ver `sacd.tscn`: el estado `idle` conserva un `timeline_length` de un cuadro y un `start_offset` en ese cuadro; el audit lo reporta como HOLD). Cualquier modelo sin idle o con idle quieto lo suma como hijo.
