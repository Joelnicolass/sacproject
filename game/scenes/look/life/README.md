# look/life

Ambient life by hour and weather: the god's swarms in the air around the camera, and swarms in the map's `LifeSpot`s
(drawn only while on screen). Nothing shows at low quality.
Edit: a swarm is `data/look/life/<name>.tres`; which swarms a god uses is the Life group of `data/look/<god>.tres`; a spot
is a `life_spot.tscn` under the map's `LifeSpots`.
