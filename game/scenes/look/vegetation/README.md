# vegetation
What: the mesh plants of a map: `FloraLayer` nodes (a species + a painted density PNG, 1 px = 8 m) and `VegetationPatch` nodes (a cluster of one species in a circle), drawn as MultiMeshes around the camera.
Not: the grass (`scenes/look/grass/`, which also owns `VegetationClear`), anything the simulation reads (nothing here exists in the dedicated or headless process), the buildings or trees of the original (`MapBuilding`).
Talks to: `FloraLayer` calls `FloraField.layers_changed` (group `flora_field`); `FloraField` (in `Look/Layers/Flora`, always processing: it plants around the camera also while the start gate pauses the tree) makes a `FloraDensity` snapshot of the layers under its `MapRoot` and hands it to one `MeshPlanter` per `VegetationLayerConfig`; `VegetationClear` calls `clears_changed()` on fields and patches (`FloraClears` hands them to the planters); `FloraField` calls `field_changed()` on patches when the preset or the switch changes; when the ground moves (`Terrain.changed`) `FloraGroundWatch` tells each `MeshPlanter` which part to plan again (`replan`, at most every `replan_every_s` once it settles) and the patches to look at the ground again; a stamp still playing (the Volcano mound, 64 s) only joins its rect each tick, and the cells it touches plan again every `FloraField.live_replan_s` (1 s; 0 = only when it settles), none outside it. The map brush (addons/terrain_tools, `DensityCanvas`) paints a layer through `FloraLayer.show_painting` (editor only) -> `FloraField.layer_painted`: a new snapshot with that layer's bytes (`FloraDensity.with_layer_bytes`) goes to the same planters (`MeshPlanter.set_flora`) and `FloraGroundWatch.mark` replans only that area; saving the PNG reimports it and `stop_painting` drops the painting. A patch reads the field's `rig` (terrain, «Vegetación» switch) and preset.
Edit: add a `FloraLayer` under the map's `Vegetation` node, drop a species from `data/vegetation/species/` (its `mesh` is a `scenes/props/meshes/<tag>.res`) and paint it: select the layer, «Flora» in the 3D bar, drag (Shift erases; «Densidad» and «Flujo» in the bar; the first click makes `maps/<mapa>/flora/<nodo>.png`), Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+S. Or paint that L8 PNG in any image editor (white = `max_per_cell`; `show_overlay` tints it in the editor). For a few plants add a `VegetationPatch` (species, `radius_m` circle, `count`, `seed`). A house brings its own `VegetationClear`; move the house and the clear moves. LOD, radius, fade: `data/look/vegetation/original_plants.tres` + the preset.

---
- `flora_density.gd` (`FloraDensity`, RefCounted): the snapshot of the layers as plain bytes, so `WorkerThreadPool` tasks read it safely. Count of a pixel = round(value x `max_per_cell` / 255).
- `flora_placement.gd` (`FloraPlacement`, funciones puras): donde van las plantas de una celda. Con la misma semilla y el mismo tag da siempre lo mismo (por eso la especie conserva el `tag` del original), sin `randi()`. Cualquier prefijo de la lista es una muestra pareja de la celda, asi que el LOD dibuja un prefijo.
- `cell_ring.gd` (`CellRing`): que celdas existen alrededor de la camara y en que tier, con histeresis. Lo usan `GrassField` y `MeshPlanter`.
- `mesh_planter.gd` (`MeshPlanter`): celdas de 32 m con un `MultiMeshInstance3D` por especie, internos y nunca guardados. Planifica en hilos (`plan_cell`, pura), arma como mucho `max_builds_per_frame` por cuadro dentro de `build_budget_ms`, cache LRU de 64, LOD por prefijo y fin por distancia con fundido.
- `patch_planner.gd` (`PatchPlanner`): las instancias de un `VegetationPatch` (puro) y su anillo del editor.
- `flora_material.gd` (`FloraMaterial`): copia por especie del material de la malla que multiplica el albedo por el color de la instancia (`vertex_color_use_as_albedo`) y desvanece con la distancia; el material compartido no se toca.
- `vegetation_species.gd` (`VegetationSpecies`), `vegetation_layer_config.gd` (`VegetationLayerConfig`), `vegetation_quality.gd` (`VegetationQuality`, por preset y `layer_id`): datos de tipo. Sombras apagadas por especie. Grupo `Variation` de la especie: `scale_range`, `height_jitter`, `tint_dark`/`tint_light`, `tint_cluster_m` (0 = por planta, >0 = manchas), `tilt_max_deg`; `FloraVariation` (`flora_variation.gd`) los aplica por hash (sin RNG) y el color va por `MultiMesh.use_colors` (16 floats por instancia).
- `flora_field.gd` (`FloraField`, `LookLayer @tool`, `flora_layer.tscn`): en el editor usa `editor_quality` (medio) y la camara del viewport. El interruptor «Vegetacion» de Opciones -> Graficos la apaga sin importar el preset.
