# look/warmup
What: behind the loading screen, every mesh, 3D material, bare spatial shader (an `@export var shader`, a script's `preload`: its material is made at runtime) and look scene (BodyLook, FxScene, or a scene of bare engine geometry like the pooled Label3D) the match can show is drawn at `Camera/WarmupStage`, a batch a frame (a look scene twice, as it is and faded: summoning, stealth and playing dead draw in the transparent pass), so the first fireball or creature does not stall compiling a pipeline; then each `WarmHook` draws what only its owner can show, and it ends when `RENDERING_INFO_PIPELINE_COMPILATIONS_DRAW + _SPECIALIZATION` stay still `settle_s` (at most `max_s`). What the walk found stays held while the match lives (a summon does not read its look from disk); the looks of every offered god's and wizard's bodies are already read beside the map (`ActiveMatch.load_paths` lists them with `WarmCollect.look_paths`). Headless (dedicated): `finished` as soon as the world is ready.
Not: loading files (the flow and `MpWorldLoader`; the walk only asks the body looks it finds to the loader's threads), the network (`scenes/net/load_report.gd` turns `progressed` / `finished` into `MpFlow.report_world_progress` / `report_world_loaded`), the screen (`scenes/ui/loading/`), choosing content by name.
Talks to: `MatchBiome.applied(god)` -> `ShaderWarmup` walks with `WarmCollect` `MatchWorld.warm_roots()` (every offered god and wizard, first: their looks start reading), the match scene and `god.look` down their typed resources (a body scene adds its `_look.tscn`, a script what it preloads) -> `WarmCollect.read_looks()` each frame until the looks are in -> `WarmStage` draws each batch: RenderingServer instances (meshes; materials and bare shaders on `probe_mesh`; the material of an ImmediateMesh a script fills, a ribbon, on two stroke probes with its vertex format, strip and triangles) and the look scenes instanced under the stage (`WarmKinds` says what is a leaf, a 3D material or a look) -> `WarmHook.ask(stage)` to every hook in `WarmHook.GROUP` (FxBus: `Bursts`/`Beams.flash_all`; atmosphere: `Weather.warm`; ambient life: `AmbientLifeLayer.warm`; each body's blink: `HitFlash.warm`) -> `finished`.
The start gate pauses the tree meanwhile: the stage always processes (the particles and skeletons of a look move), FxBus's flashed bursts process while they flash, and what follows the camera (the FollowCamera of the rain and of the air life, the Flora field) always processes, so the paused world is drawn as in play.
Edit: `match.tscn` › `Warmup` (`batch_size`, `scene_draws`, `faded`, `settle_s`, `max_s`, `probe_mesh`; defaults in `shader_warmup.tscn`) and `Camera/WarmupStage` (drag it with the gizmo: it must stay in view). Something the walk cannot see (made at runtime, or drawn only by its owner): add a `WarmHook` to its owner's scene and connect `asked` to a method that draws it for one frame with no sound, no shake and no `played`.
