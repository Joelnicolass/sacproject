# look/warmup
What: behind the loading screen, every mesh, 3D material and look scene (BodyLook, FxScene) the match can show is drawn once at `Camera/WarmupStage`, a batch a frame, so the first fireball or creature does not stall compiling a pipeline; then each `WarmHook` draws what only its owner can show, and it ends when `RENDERING_INFO_PIPELINE_COMPILATIONS_DRAW + _SPECIALIZATION` stay still `settle_s` (at most `max_s`). Headless (dedicated): `finished` as soon as the world is ready.
Not: loading files (the flow and `MpWorldLoader`), the network (`scenes/net/load_report.gd` turns `progressed` / `finished` into `MpFlow.report_world_progress` / `report_world_loaded`), the screen (`scenes/ui/loading/`), choosing content by name.
Talks to: `MatchBiome.applied(god)` -> `ShaderWarmup` walks with `WarmCollect` the match scene, `god.look` and `MatchWorld.warm_roots()` (the map, every offered god and wizard) down their typed resources (a body scene adds its `_look.tscn`) -> draws with RenderingServer instances (meshes, materials on `probe_mesh`) and instances the look scenes under the stage -> `WarmHook.ask(stage)` to every hook in `WarmHook.GROUP` (FxBus: `Bursts`/`Beams.flash_all`; atmosphere: `Weather.warm`) -> `finished`.
Edit: `match.tscn` › `Warmup` (`batch_size`, `settle_s`, `max_s`, `probe_mesh`) and `Camera/WarmupStage` (drag it with the gizmo: it must stay in view). Something the walk cannot see (made at runtime): add a `WarmHook` to its owner's scene and connect `asked` to a method that draws it for one frame with no sound, no shake and no `played`.
Check: `agent/runs/run_warmup.sh` (W5: the compilation counters do not move after `world_loaded`) · headless: `rules_suite` and `run_net_quick.sh` load the match without waiting.
