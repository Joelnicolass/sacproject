# map/terrain
What: the ground of a map, the packed scene `terrain.tscn` that every map instances. Its root `Terrain` is the one owner and the whole API: it answers how high the land is, where it has holes, its tiles and its size, and it is the only writer of how far the ground moved in the match (`GroundField`: what stays, the stamps that play now, the vertices taken away). The one source is the map's `heights.png` + `void.png` (the 256² lattice, imported as `TerrainHeights`); `TerrainShape` derives the triangles (`TerrainFaces`, land and cliff walls, `MapTerrainData` in memory, never saved) and their index (`TerrainRules`) on load, and again in the editor once a reimport or a brush stroke has been still for 0.4 s. Its children: `Mesh` (`TerrainMesh`) builds the mesh from those triangles (never headless or on the dedicated server), `Collision` (`TerrainCollision`) is the body to stand on in chunks of 8x8 cells, `Live` (`TerrainLive`) gathers the stamps that play and hands their shifts to Terrain once per physics tick, `Net` (`TerrainNet`) carries what stays to the clients, `Status` (`TerrainStatus`) shows the live state, `HoleWalls`, `GroundScars`, `Missing`. `GroundRay` reads the height a physics ray finds (roofs and units included).
Not: where a building stands (MapBuilding), the look of the ground (look/ground, which moves the mesh's vertices with a texture), pathing (map/nav), what a spell does (spells/zones), where the editor shows the map (`MapRoot.editor_recenter`).
Talks to: everyone asks `Terrain.of(node)` (`height_at`, `normal_at`, `is_on_ground`, `bounds`, `lowest`, `sampler()` for worker threads; `TerrainRules` is the arithmetic) or holds a `terrain: Terrain` socket. Whatever moves the ground asks it and knows no lattice: a `TerrainStamp` calls `stamp`/`unstamp` (every peer, from its own clock; they only note the request, `Live` evaluates every stamp of the tick at once and Terrain writes them as differences, runs `StampCarry` once on their union and emits `changed` once) and `keep_stamp` (server), a picture stamp gets its pattern once (`StampRules.pattern_under`); `HoleCutter` calls `cut_hole`/`fill_hole` (server; `HoleRules` says which vertex may go). What stays is told twice: `changed(rect)` (every change, live ones too: `TerrainCollision`, `look/ground/TerrainDeformTexture`) and `settled(rect)` (a kept stamp, a stamp's end, a hole, a snapshot; in the editor a brush stroke: `FloraField` replans and replants only the patches the rect touches, the collision remakes at once); a hole also emits `holes_changed(rect)`; `NavService` bakes again on `holes_changed` and `kept` only (a live stamp that ends leaves nothing). On the server Terrain emits `kept`/`hole_set`; `Net` sends them (RPC, and an `MpKit` snapshot to a seat that rejoins within `rejoin_grace_s`: nobody joins a match half way) and on a client hands them back through `keep`/`set_hole`/`load_snapshot`, which ignore what this peer already has. `sampler()` copies the field only when it settled, never per live tick. `derived` tells `Mesh` the triangles were made again. `NavFingerprint` hashes the heights.
Edit: on the map scene, the `Terrain` instance: `heights` (the map's heights.png; its height step is «metres_per_unit» in the Import dock), `tiles` (tiles.png as Image), `ground_roles` (ground_roles.png), `light_map` (light.png); `Terrain/Mesh`: `material_override` (the plain grey ground with no look; a god's ground is worn from `Look/Layers/Ground` and never saved), `smooth_ground`; `Terrain/Collision`: `chunk_cells`, `refresh_s` (how long a chunk under a playing stamp may wait; what settles is remade at once); `Terrain/Live.min_step_m` (a stamp is redrawn only when its height moved that much or its ring travelled that far); `Terrain/Status.show_view` (a picture of the shift). The shape of the land: paint heights.png and void.png in any image editor, or Blender (tools/README.md). A new map's ground: tools/import/scaffold_map.py writes a flat heights.png and the instance. A stamp's radius and peak are the `Reach` cylinder of its `TerrainStamp` (gizmo), its shape the curves of its `StampProfile` (or a picture, `heightmap`, plus `flatten`); `manual` + `leave_now()` leaves the mark at once (DropDent), `stop()` takes a live stamp back.
Check: debug/spells/cast_terrain.tscn (F6: Bore and Erupt over the altar, cut Erupt, Volcano with pyro; Remote → `Terrain/Status`: `rev`, `live_stamps` (2 keys per Quake), `holes`) · debug/units/f132_quake_onhit.tscn (F6: the Trogg follows the sinking land) · rules_suite `terrain_cases` (`zone_run.tscn -- --only=terrain`, `--case=quake_ground|carry_quake|carry_dent|carry_roof|base_hole|base_pin|rim_stamp|void_stamp|erupt_cancel|soul_hole|nav_trigger|net_inflight|net_rejoin_mid_volcano|net_quake_client`) and `grid_constants` · debug/net/terrain_late_join.tscn (headless, one process: server, clients and a seat that rejoins 20 s into a Volcano, `TERRAIN NET PASS`) · debug/perf/terrain_deform_bench.tscn (window: 5 Quakes at once add <= 1 ms per tick, no frame over 20 ms, `TERRAIN DEFORM BENCH PASS`) · debug/map/ground_dump.gd (the answers bit by bit, before against after) · debug/terrain/load_time.gd (how long a map takes to load, `--log=terrain_load` for the parts) · debug/map/terrain_shape.tscn, debug/map/building_gallery.tscn.

Who moves the ground (RFC-057, rfcs/RFC-057-deformable-terrain.md). Every one is a node in its own scene, no code per spell:

| Power | Kind | Node → API |
|---|---|---|
| Erupt | live; cut short, it goes at once | `erupt_zone.tscn`: `TerrainStamp` Mound, Rebound, Wave → `stamp`/`unstamp`; `Cut` (`ZoneStampStop`) → `stop()` |
| Quake (Jabberocky, Bovine Intervention's cow) | live, 0.5 s | `quake_ground.tscn` (Rebound + Wave) in `quake_wave.tscn` on the server; a copy with `on_clients_only` in `quake_ring.tscn` for a client's look |
| Volcano | live mound that stays at 25 %, plus a crater | `volcano_zone.tscn`: Mound (`keep`), Crater (`keep`) → `stamp`, then `keep_stamp` |
| Bombardment | a dent that stays | `meteor.tscn`: Dent (`manual`, `keep`), `DropDent` → `leave_now()` → `keep_stamp` |
| Bore | holes | `HoleCutter` → `cut_hole`/`fill_hole`; `HoleRules` (rim, 11 m from a part, never under a base) |

What follows the ground: `StampCarry` puts the bodies standing on the land back on it, up and down (server); a `GroundFootprint` with `pins_ground` keeps its base level under any stamp, with a soft ring (`PinRules`, worked out once in `Terrain._ready`); a soul over a hole falls and is freed `vanish_depth_m` below its last ground. Add a power that bends the land: a `TerrainStamp` (or a `HoleCutter`) in its scene; if that scene lives only on the server, a copy with `on_clients_only` in its FX.

The API (063D-2b): `Terrain` is the answers and the writer in one node (it merged the two that were). Nothing outside `scenes/map/terrain/` sees a lattice `Rect2i` or a vertex id: `stamp(key, profile, center, reach_m, peak_m, at_s, pattern)`, `unstamp(key)`, `keep_stamp(key, profile, center, reach_m, peak_m, pattern)`, `cut_hole(center, radius_m)` and `fill_hole(center, radius_m)` take metres; the holes come back as points (y = the land there, never NAN) and `FallingChunk` gets `params {"at"}`. `field()` (GroundField) and `index` (TerrainRules.Index) are read only, for the walls, the falling piece, the deform texture and the tests. The building parts a hole keeps away from are `HoleRules.parts_of(tree)`.

The lattice (063D-2a): `TerrainGrid` is the one place of the grid's numbers (`SIDE` 256 vertices per side, `CELL_M` 10 m, `SIZE_M`, `COUNT`) and of the arithmetic between a point in metres, a vertex (column, row) and its id (`vertex_id`, `id_of`, `vertex_of`, `point_of`, `vertex_near`, `vertex_rect`, `area_of`). The ground shaders keep one mirror (`DEFORM_CELL`, `DEFORM_LAST` in look/ground/terrain_deform.gdshaderinc); `debug/suite/grid_constants_cases.gd` (rules_suite `grid_constants`) fails when they differ.

The one entry point (063-1, 063D-2c): `bounds()` is the map's footprint in metres (x and z >= 0), `field().version` counts how many times the ground moved, `mesh` is the node that draws it. The look layer (`LookRig.terrain`), the minimap (`MinimapTerrainBake.terrain`), `NavService.terrain` and `MapRoot.terrain` point at the Terrain, not at the mesh; grass, minimap and the marks size themselves from `bounds()`.

Marks on the ground (061k-5): `GroundScars` (child of `Terrain`, in every map) is the one owner of the burns, cracks and craters painted into the ground: 64 x 64 tiles of 10 m in a `Texture2DArray` plus an index with one texel per tile (the map's footprint over 10 m), both shader globals (`scar_index`, `scar_tiles`) that `ground_scars.gdshaderinc` reads in the ground shader; `GroundScarRules` is the arithmetic (tiles touched, oldest tile out, height filter). The limit is the option «Cantidad de marcas» (32-256). Presentation only: headless it holds nothing. Effects ask for a mark with a `ScarMark` (scenes/spells/scar_mark.gd). Every peer paints on its own when it sees the effect (nothing travels per mark; blood is placed at random per peer, so two players can differ by a tile or two); `GroundScars.marks` (`GroundScarRecord`) keeps what was painted, in order, and its child `Net` (`GroundScarsNet`) hands that list in one MpKit snapshot to a player who comes back after a drop: the host's own list, or on a dedicated server (which draws nothing) the list of a player already in, asked by RPC; the peer cleans its tiles and paints it again. Check: `debug/look/scars_lab.tscn` (F6: click to paint, 1..9 picks the mark, F throws a burst to see the oldest tiles go) · rules_suite `scar_cases` (`scar_record` for the list; or `debug/suite/scar_cases.tscn` alone).

Holes (061k-3, Bore): a vertex taken away (`Terrain.cut_hole`) is a real hole: no land triangle that touches it, in `height_at`, in the collision (the same tick: `Terrain.holes_changed`), in the navmesh (`NavService` bakes again, gathering the holes of 2 s) and on screen (the ground shader discards the triangles, `HoleWalls` draws the pit with the cliff texture, `FallingChunk` drops the piece). `HoleRules` says when a vertex may go (not the rim, not one that is gone, not within 11 m of a building, not under a base: `HoleRules.bases_of`); `HoleWallRules` and `FallingChunkRules` are the arrays of the walls and of the piece. A hole is a little bigger than the original's in two of its four cells (the original keeps half a cell there; here the triangles that touch the vertex all go). Check: rules_suite `terrain_cases` (`--case=bore_rules|bore_walls|bore_cut|bore_cancel|bore_nav`).

Walls under the rim (the map's edge, the pits, a falling piece): `WallUvRules` puts the cliff texture in the mesh UV (x = metres along the rim, continuous from wall to wall; y = 0 at the rim, 1 at the foot) for `TerrainMeshArrays`, `HoleWallRules` and `FallingChunkRules`; the ground shaders read it through `look/ground/ground_edge.gdshaderinc` (`edge_repeat_m`, mirrored repeat). What is under the island is the look's `Underside` layer (look/sky). Check: match_shots `--views=rim,overview` + `tools/look/measure_void.py`.
