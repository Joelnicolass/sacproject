# map/terrain
What: the ground of a map. `Terrain` (today the child `Shape` of the mesh) is the one owner and the whole API: it answers how high the land is, where it has holes, its tiles and its size, and it is the only writer of how far the ground moved in the match (`GroundField`: what stays, the stamps that play now, the vertices taken away). `TerrainMesh` shows it, `TerrainCollision` (child `Collision`) is the body to stand on in chunks of 8x8 cells, `TerrainNet` (child `Net`) carries what stays to the clients, `TerrainStatus` (`Shape/Status`) shows the live state, `GroundRay` reads the height a physics ray finds (roofs and units included).
Not: where a building stands (MapBuilding), the look of the ground (look/ground, which moves the mesh's vertices with a texture), pathing (map/nav), what a spell does (spells/zones).
Talks to: everyone asks `Terrain.of(node)` (`height_at`, `normal_at`, `is_on_ground`, `bounds`, `lowest`, `sampler()` for worker threads; `TerrainRules` is the arithmetic) or holds a `terrain: Terrain` socket. Whatever moves the ground asks it and knows no lattice: a `TerrainStamp` calls `stamp`/`unstamp` (every peer, from its own clock) and `keep_stamp` (server), a picture stamp gets its `stamp_pattern` once; `HoleCutter` calls `cut_hole`/`fill_hole` (server; `HoleRules` says which vertex may go). Terrain sends what stays through `Net` (RPC, and an `MpKit` snapshot to a late joiner) and applies what `Net` tells on the clients; then it emits `changed(rect)` and, for a hole, `holes_changed(rect)`: `TerrainCollision`, `look/ground/TerrainDeformTexture`, `look/vegetation/FloraField` and `NavService` listen.
Edit: on the map scene, `Terrain/Shape`: `data` (MapTerrainData), `tiles` (the map's tiles.png as Image), `mesh` and `net` sockets; `Shape/Status.show_view` (a picture of the shift); on `Terrain`: `ground_roles`, `light_map`, `material_override`, `smooth_ground`; on `Terrain/Collision`: `chunk_cells`, `refresh_s`. A stamp's radius and peak are the `Reach` cylinder of its `TerrainStamp` (gizmo), its shape the curves of its `StampProfile` (or a picture, `heightmap`, plus `flatten`); `min_step_m` keeps a slow decline from being redrawn each tick, `manual` + `leave_now()` leaves the mark at once (DropDent), `stop()` takes a live stamp back.
Check: debug/spells/cast_terrain.tscn (F6; Remote → `Terrain/Shape/Status`: `rev`, `live_stamps`, `holes`) · rules_suite `terrain_cases` (`zone_run.tscn -- --only=terrain`) and `grid_constants` · debug/net/terrain_late_join.tscn (headless, one process: server and two clients, `TERRAIN NET PASS`) · debug/map/ground_dump.gd (the answers bit by bit, before against after) · debug/map/terrain_shape.tscn, debug/map/building_gallery.tscn.

---

The API (063D-2b): `Terrain` is the answers and the writer in one node (it merged the two that were). Nothing outside `scenes/map/terrain/` sees a lattice `Rect2i` or a vertex id: `stamp(key, profile, center, reach_m, peak_m, at_s, pattern)`, `unstamp(key)`, `keep_stamp(key, profile, center, reach_m, peak_m, pattern)`, `stamp_pattern(profile, center, reach_m, peak_m)`, `cut_hole(center, radius_m)` and `fill_hole(center, radius_m)` take metres; the holes come back as points (y = the land there, never NAN) and `FallingChunk` gets `params {"at"}`. `field()` (GroundField) and `index` (TerrainRules.Index) are read only, for the walls, the falling piece, the deform texture and the tests. The building parts a hole keeps away from are `HoleRules.parts_of(tree)`. The interim tree (until 063D-3 makes `terrain.tscn`): `Terrain` (TerrainMesh, the glb) › `Shape` (Terrain) › `Status`, and `Net`, `Collision`, `GroundScars`, `HoleWalls` beside `Shape`.

The lattice (063D-2a): `TerrainGrid` is the one place of the grid's numbers (`SIDE` 256 vertices per side, `CELL_M` 10 m, `SIZE_M`, `COUNT`) and of the arithmetic between a point in metres, a vertex (column, row) and its id (`vertex_id`, `id_of`, `vertex_of`, `point_of`, `vertex_near`, `vertex_rect`, `area_of`). The ground shaders keep one mirror (`DEFORM_CELL`, `DEFORM_LAST` in look/ground/terrain_deform.gdshaderinc); `debug/suite/grid_constants_cases.gd` (rules_suite `grid_constants`) fails when they differ.

The one entry point (063-1, 063D-2c): `bounds()` is the map's footprint in metres (x and z >= 0), `field().version` counts how many times the ground moved, `mesh` is the node that draws it. The look layer (`LookRig.terrain`), the minimap (`MinimapTerrainBake.terrain`), `NavService.terrain` and `MapRoot.terrain` point at the Terrain, not at the mesh; grass, minimap and the marks size themselves from `bounds()`.

Marks on the ground (061k-5): `GroundScars` (beside `Shape`, in every map) is the one owner of the burns, cracks and craters painted into the ground: 64 x 64 tiles of 10 m in a `Texture2DArray` plus an index with one texel per tile (the map's footprint over 10 m), both shader globals (`scar_index`, `scar_tiles`) that `ground_scars.gdshaderinc` reads in the two ground shaders; `GroundScarRules` is the arithmetic (tiles touched, oldest tile out, height filter). The limit is the option «Cantidad de marcas» (32-256). Presentation only: headless it holds nothing. Effects ask for a mark with a `ScarMark` (scenes/spells/scar_mark.gd). Check: `debug/look/scars_lab.tscn` (F6: click to paint, 1..9 picks the mark, F throws a burst to see the oldest tiles go) · rules_suite `scar_cases` (or `debug/suite/scar_cases.tscn` alone).

Holes (061k-3, Bore): a vertex taken away (`Terrain.cut_hole`) is a real hole: no land triangle that touches it, in `height_at`, in the collision (the same tick: `Terrain.holes_changed`), in the navmesh (`NavService` bakes again, gathering the holes of 2 s) and on screen (the ground shader discards the triangles, `HoleWalls` draws the pit with the cliff texture, `FallingChunk` drops the piece). `HoleRules` says when a vertex may go (not the rim, not one that is gone, not within 11 m of a building); `HoleWallRules` and `FallingChunkRules` are the arrays of the walls and of the piece. A hole is a little bigger than the original's in two of its four cells (the original keeps half a cell there; here the triangles that touch the vertex all go). Check: rules_suite `terrain_cases` (`--case=bore_rules|bore_walls|bore_cut|bore_cancel|bore_nav`).

Walls under the rim (the map's edge, the pits, a falling piece): `WallUvRules` puts the cliff texture in the mesh UV (x = metres along the rim, continuous from wall to wall; y = 0 at the rim, 1 at the foot) for `TerrainMeshArrays`, `HoleWallRules` and `FallingChunkRules`; the ground shaders read it through `look/ground/ground_edge.gdshaderinc` (`edge_repeat_m`, mirrored repeat). A terrain.glb imported before `TerrainMeshArrays.VERSION` is rebuilt in memory with a warning until it is reimported. What is under the island is the look's `Underside` layer (look/sky). Check: match_shots `--views=rim,overview` + `tools/look/measure_void.py`.
