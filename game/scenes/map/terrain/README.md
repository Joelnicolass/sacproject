# map/terrain
What: the ground of a map. `GroundSurface` (child `Shape`) owns the numbers: heights, holes, tiles, lowest land, and how far the ground has moved since loading (`GroundField`: what stays, the stamps that play now, the vertices taken away); `Terrain` shows it; `TerrainCollision` (child `Collision`) is the body to stand on, in chunks of 8x8 cells; `GroundRay` reads the surface height a physics ray finds (roofs and units included).
Not: where a building stands (MapBuilding), the look of the ground (look/ground, which moves the vertices of the mesh with a texture), pathing (map/nav), what a spell does (spells/zones).
Talks to: everyone asks `GroundSurface.of(node).height_at(x, z)` (`TerrainRules` is the pure arithmetic, also behind `shape.sampler()` for worker threads); `TerrainStamp` nodes (inside a spell's zone) play a `StampProfile` and ask the only writer, `TerrainDeformer`, to show it (`set_live`) or keep it (`commit`, server only; clients get the same bytes by RPC, a late joiner the whole ground in an `MpKit` snapshot); the deformer tells `GroundSurface.changed(rect)`, and `TerrainCollision`, `look/ground/TerrainDeformTexture` and `look/vegetation/FloraField` listen.
Edit: `data` (MapTerrainData) and `tiles_path` on the map scene's Terrain/Shape; `ground_roles` (the map's ground_roles.png: which kind of ground lies where), `light_map` (its light.png), `material_override` and `smooth_ground` on Terrain; `chunk_cells` and `refresh_s` on Terrain/Collision. A stamp's radius and peak are the `Reach` cylinder of its `TerrainStamp` (gizmo), its shape the curves of its `StampProfile`. A stamp can be a picture instead of curves (`StampProfile.heightmap`, one pixel per 10 m, plus `flatten` toward the height of its center): the server works its `pattern` out once from the land under it (`StampRules.ground_under`/`pattern`) and the zone sends it to the clients; `TerrainStamp.carries` lifts the bodies the ground comes up through (`StampCarry`; Volcano's mound), `min_step_m` keeps a slow decline from being redrawn at every tick, and `stop()` takes a live stamp back at once.
Check: debug/map/terrain_shape.tscn (headless, prints TERRAIN SHAPE PASS), debug/map/building_gallery.tscn · debug/spells/cast_terrain.tscn (F6) · rules_suite `terrain_cases` (`zone_run.tscn -- --only=terrain`) · debug/net/terrain_late_join.tscn (headless, one process: server and two clients, `TERRAIN NET PASS`).

---

The one entry point (063-1): `GroundSurface` is the only thing that answers `height_at`, `is_on_ground`, `normal_at`, `bounds()` (the map's footprint in metres, x and z >= 0), `version()` (how many times the ground moved) and `meshes` (the nodes that draw it: the `Terrain` adds itself, a ground drawn by something else lists them in the inspector). `field()` is the `GroundField`, the lattice of displacement and holes with its own `size` and `cell_m`. The look layer (`LookRig.ground`), the minimap (`MinimapTerrainBake.surface`), `NavService.ground` and `MapRoot.ground` point at the surface, not at the mesh; grass, minimap and the marks size themselves from `bounds()`.

Marks on the ground (061k-5): `GroundScars` (child of `Terrain`, in every map) is the one owner of the burns, cracks and craters painted into the ground: 64 x 64 tiles of 10 m in a `Texture2DArray` plus an index with one texel per tile (the map's footprint over 10 m), both shader globals (`scar_index`, `scar_tiles`) that `ground_scars.gdshaderinc` reads in the two ground shaders; `GroundScarRules` is the arithmetic (tiles touched, oldest tile out, height filter). The limit is the option «Cantidad de marcas» (32-256). Presentation only: headless it holds nothing. Effects ask for a mark with a `ScarMark` (scenes/spells/scar_mark.gd). Check: `debug/look/scars_lab.tscn` (F6: click to paint, 1..9 picks the mark, F throws a burst to see the oldest tiles go) · rules_suite `scar_cases` (or `debug/suite/scar_cases.tscn` alone).

Holes (061k-3, Bore): a vertex taken away (`TerrainDeformer.set_removed`) is a real hole: no land triangle that touches it, in `height_at`, in the collision (the same tick: `GroundSurface.holes_changed`), in the navmesh (`NavService` bakes again, gathering the holes of 2 s) and on screen (the ground shader discards the triangles, `HoleWalls` draws the pit with the cliff texture, `FallingChunk` drops the piece). `HoleRules` says when a vertex may go (not the rim, not one that is gone, not within 11 m of a building); `HoleWallRules` and `FallingChunkRules` are the arrays of the walls and of the piece. A hole is a little bigger than the original's in two of its four cells (the original keeps half a cell there; here the triangles that touch the vertex all go). Check: rules_suite `terrain_cases` (`--case=bore_rules|bore_walls|bore_cut|bore_cancel|bore_nav`).

Walls under the rim (the map's edge, the pits, a falling piece): `WallUvRules` puts the cliff texture in the mesh UV (x = metres along the rim, continuous from wall to wall; y = 0 at the rim, 1 at the foot) for `TerrainMeshArrays`, `HoleWallRules` and `FallingChunkRules`; the ground shaders read it through `look/ground/ground_edge.gdshaderinc` (`edge_repeat_m`, mirrored repeat). A terrain.glb imported before `TerrainMeshArrays.VERSION` is rebuilt in memory with a warning until it is reimported. What is under the island is the look's `Underside` layer (look/sky). Check: match_shots `--views=rim,overview` + `tools/look/measure_void.py`.
