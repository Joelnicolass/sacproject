# spells
What: casting a spell: `Spellbook` (what the wizard knows, cooldowns), `Caster` (checks, mana, timing), one `SpellAction` per spell.
Not: the wizard body (`scenes/wizards/`), the spell data (`data/catalog/spells/<tag>.tres`, a `SpellType`), what the AI wants to cast (`scenes/ai/`).
Talks to: HUD/input call `Caster.request_cast/request_summon/request_structure`; the host runs `submit_*`; `Caster.feedback/cast_fx_changed` go up to the HUD.
Talks to: `Caster` calls down to a `SpellAction` (`check`, `begin`, `finish`), which spawns projectiles/effects through `SpellSpawner` and hits units via `Health`.
Edit: `data/catalog/spells/<tag>.tres` (cost, range, cooldown, `action` scene) · `actions/<name>_action.tscn` (the behaviour) · projectile/effect scenes in this folder.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_*.tscn` (F6; `cast_wards.tscn` for the level-2 wards, `cast_souls.tscn` for the soul spells, `cast_control.tscn` for control on one foe, `cast_walls.tscn` for the walls), `guardian_altar.tscn`, `wrath_splash.tscn` · rules_suite `ward_cases`, `control_cases`, `soul_cases`, `animate_dead_cases`, `wall_cases` · `match_shots --cast=niks|dlhs|mrfe|mrff|rwsp|zerf|eniv|rapf|htel|pmcd|deda|dnws` (`--views=foe_close` for control).
Control on one foe (Freeze, Grasping Vines, Rings of Fire, Slime): `AfflictAction` (one script, a scene per spell) asks its `SpellTarget` (embedded Resource: who, ground only, refuses ccProtected) and lays the state on the foe's `Afflictions`; `SlimeBall` is the ball that falls while Slime is cast. Check: `debug/spells/cast_control.tscn` (F6) · rules_suite `control_cases` · `match_shots --cast=zerf|eniv|rapf|htel --views=foe_close` (prints each state on the closest foe).
Zones on the ground (Explosion, Frozen Ground, Demonic Rift): `ZoneAction` (one script, a scene per spell) spawns a `GroundZone` through `SpellSpawner` when the cast starts, releases it when it ends and cancels it when it is cut; see `zones/README.md`. Check: `debug/spells/cast_zones.tscn` (F6) · rules_suite `zone_cases`/`rift_cases`/`aura_cases` · `match_shots --cast=pxef|zorf|tfir|ruah --views=zone,foe_close`.
Healing Aura (`ruah`) is a `WardAction` with a `target` (the creature that carries it): the ward lives in every body (`wards/healing_aura.tscn`).
Wards (shields and temporary forms): see `wards/README.md`. Spells that fetch souls (Soul Mole, Soul Wind): `carrier/README.md`; Animate Dead's effect: `animate_dead/README.md`.
Jumping spells (Chain Lightning, Rainbow): `chain/README.md`. Dragonfire's dragon is a guided `SpellCarrier` (`carrier/README.md`). Halo of Earth is `units/abilities/halo_of_earth.gd` (the same node as the Rhinok's); check them with `debug/suite/chain_cases.tscn` and `match_shots --level=5 --cast=ntlc|wobr|rifd|olah`. The five walls (Firewall, Wall of Spikes, Wailing Wall, Vinewall, Fence): `walls/README.md`.
Known debt: `SpellType` still has raw `flags*` and unit-less fields; 4 actions are at inheritance depth 2 (see `CONVENTIONS.md`).
