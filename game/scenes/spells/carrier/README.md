# spells/carrier
What: something a spell sends to a soul and back (Soul Mole's rock, Soul Wind's funnel). `SpellCarrier` walks the route (`CarrierPath`: two curves that bend to one side, 270 ticks out and 270 back), `SoulFetch` takes the soul at the turn, `CarrierPulse` stuns (and maybe hurts) what it passes within its `Reach`, the rest of the children are the look.
Not: casting (`SoulAction` + `Caster`), who collects the soul at the end (`SoulSystem`), the stun itself (`Stunned`), the damage maths (`Health`).
Talks to: `SoulAction.started` asks `SpellSpawner` for a carrier (kind `SoulMole` / `SoulWind`) and calls `arm()` when the casting ends or `stop()` if it is cut; the carrier emits `reached(soul)` and `ended`; `SoulFetch` and `CarrierPulse` listen to / read their carrier (they are children, they never name it by path); the soul follows `Soul.carrier`.
Edit: `soul_mole.tscn` / `soul_wind.tscn` (Reach radius with the gizmo, `stun_ticks`, `damage_foes`, the look nodes, `roundtrip_ticks`, `bend`) · `data/fx/soul_*.tres` (particles) · `data/fx/beams/soul_wind_bolt.tres` (the bolt) · `funnel.gdshader` (wind speed, turn, brightness). Another spell that travels (Charm, Dragonfire...) = another carrier scene with other children. The funnel mesh is baked by `tools/bake/make_soul_wind_funnel.gd`.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_souls.tscn` (F6, `spell_N` then click a soul) · rules_suite `soul_cases` · `match_shots --cast=pmcd|dnws`.
