# spells/carrier
What: something a spell sends on a trip. One `SpellCarrier`, `motion` says which: SOUL_TRIP (Soul Mole's rock, Soul Wind's funnel) walks the route of `CarrierPath` to a soul and back (270 ticks each way), `SoulFetch` takes the soul at the turn, `CarrierPulse` stuns (and maybe hurts) what it passes within its `Reach`; GUIDED (Dragonfire's dragon, `dragonfire.tscn`) climbs a spiral around the wizard while he casts and then flies to its target (`GuidedFlight`), and its `OnArrive` (`DragonArrive`, a `CarrierArrive`) burns what it crosses, hurts and sets ablaze at each target and asks its `ChainHop` for the next (5 targets); with none it shrinks away. The rest of the children are the look (`dragon_look.tscn`: the `dfir` model with 5 frames as blend shapes).
Not: casting (`SoulAction` / `CarrierAction` + `Caster`), who collects the soul at the end (`SoulSystem`), who is next in a chain (`chain/ChainHop`), the stun itself (`Stunned`), the damage maths (`Health`).
Talks to: the action asks `SpellSpawner` for a carrier (kind `SoulMole` / `SoulWind` / `Dragonfire`) and calls `arm()` when the casting ends or `stop()` if it is cut (a guided one also gets `begin(...)` when the cast starts); the carrier emits `reached(soul)` and `ended`; its children listen to / read it (they never name it by path); the soul follows `Soul.carrier`. A guided carrier replicates `position`, `rotation` and `scale`.
Edit: `soul_mole.tscn` / `soul_wind.tscn` (Reach radius with the gizmo, `stun_s`, `damage_foes`, the look nodes, `roundtrip_s`, `bend`) · `dragonfire.tscn` (`turn_rate`, `flying_height_m`, `shrink_s`, the `Chain` hits, `OnArrive` burn and wander knobs, the flame emitters at the tail) · `dragon_look.tscn` and `dragon.gdshader` (energy 20, opacity 0.5; the wing animation) · `data/fx/soul_*.tres` and `data/fx/spells/dragon_*.tres` (particles) · `data/fx/beams/soul_wind_bolt.tres` · `funnel.gdshader`. Each carrier scene has a `Glow` (`StateLight`, `lit_on_ready`) that travels with it. Another spell that travels = another carrier scene with other children. The funnel mesh is baked by `tools/bake/make_soul_wind_funnel.gd`; the dragon by `tools/import/import_dragonfire.py` (Blender CLI).
Level 8 (061i): the same carrier with four more parts. GUIDED + `CarrierHold` + `TargetEffect`: `charm.tscn` (spirits circle the wizard, fly, circle the creature 5 s, it changes side), `blind_rage.tscn` (the fist forms 82 ticks over the land, a ring grows to 30 m, every creature within is enraged 30 s), `vaporization.tscn` (the shot, guts that circle, `HoldBody` stuns and swells, `GibEffect`). FALL (`CarrierFall`): `bovine_intervention.tscn` (born buried 15 m ahead when the cast ends, 12 s arc of 200 m, `GibEffect` on arriving, `CarrierLanding` quake). HUNT (`CarrierHunt`): `death.tscn` (rises out of the land, hunts the closest non-wizard creature, `KillEffect`, `SpellType.count` kills or `duration_s`). The look follows `phase` (`MorphModel` plays the frames of the original as blend shapes; `SwarmOrbit`, `GrowingRing`, `PhaseSprite`, `PhaseCue`, `PhaseBurst`). Lights: each carrier has an `ActorLight` of the dominant color of its texture; the bursts have an `FxLightSpec`.
