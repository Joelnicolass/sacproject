# spells/carrier
What: something a spell sends flying: `SpellCarrier` rises in a spiral around the wizard while he casts, flies to its target when the casting ends (a body it follows, or a point), and asks its `CarrierArrive` what to do on the way and on arriving. Dragonfire's dragon (`dragonfire.tscn`: the `dfir` model with 5 frames as blend shapes in `dragon_look.tscn`) is the first one: `DragonArrive` burns what it crosses, hurts and sets ablaze at each target and asks its `ChainHop` for the next (5 targets); with none it shrinks away.
Not: casting (`CarrierAction` + `Caster`), who is next (`chain/ChainHop`), how a hit hurts (`Health`).
Talks to: `CarrierAction.started` makes it through `SpellSpawner` (kind `Dragonfire`) so every peer draws it; `position`, `rotation` and `scale` are replicated, the server moves it and runs `DragonArrive`; sounds and fire sprites hang from the scene.
Edit: `dragonfire.tscn` (`turn_rate`, `flying_height_m`, `shrink_s`, the `Chain` hits, `OnArrive` burn and wander knobs, the flame emitters at the tail) · `dragon_look.tscn` and `dragon.gdshader` (energy 20, opacity 0.5; the wing animation) · `data/catalog/spells/rifd.tres`.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_chain.tscn` (F6; `Foes.wizard_god` pyro, click the first Trogg) · `debug/suite/chain_cases.tscn` (`chain_dragon*`) · `match_shots --cast=rifd`. The model is made by `tools/import/import_dragonfire.py` (Blender CLI).
