# spells/drops
What: what a cloud lets go and what happens where it ends. A `ZoneDrop` flies under a pull of gravity (a frog, a drop of fire or of plague, a meteorite), rests on the ground or sticks to a body, and its children say the rest: `DropBlast` (damage, fire, stun, bursts), `DropPoison` (a contagious dose), `FrogHop` + `FrogCling` (a frog that jumps and sticks, ×0.6 pace each) and `FrogLook` (its frame). `DropDent` leaves a permanent dent where a drop ends: the meteorite's `Dent` is a `manual` TerrainStamp (Reach 15 m radius, 1.2 m deep, half-circle profile) that `DropDent` leaves at once (`TerrainStamp.leave_now`, kept by the map's Terrain on the server); the dents add up.
Not: where and when a drop is born (the cloud's `ZoneDropper` and `DropLauncher`, in `zones/`), the cloud itself (`CloudLook`), the spell's numbers (they travel with the drop from the `SpellType`).
Talks to: `DropLauncher` spawns the drop with `SpellSpawner` (a `projectiles` kind: the drop points at no spell); `ZoneDrop.landed/touched/ended` go up to its children; the server moves it and replicates `position` and `status`, every peer draws it; the blast and the poison reach everybody through `FxBus.broadcast` (the sounds, shakes and lights are the ones of each burst's name).
Edit: `rain_frog.tscn`, `fire_drop.tscn`, `plague_drop.tscn`, `meteor.tscn`, `lava_ball.tscn` (the Volcano's: shot at the spell's speed leaning up for gravity, explodes on the first body or the ground) (gravity, contact box, bursts, fire, stun, dose, sprite) · `data/fx/frog_*`, `fireball_*`, `plague_*`, `meteor_*`, `fire_drop_trail` · cues in `data/audio/cues/`. To add a drop: a scene with a `ZoneDrop` root, its effect children, a kind in `SpellSpawner.projectiles`, and a `DropLauncher` in a cloud.
Not drawn: the scorch of the bolts and the meteorites (SCAR has no reader), the ash the exe puts in the air around Rain of Fire (the amount is NOT VERIFIED), the dynamic lights of the exe (the glow is additive sprites and `FxLightSpec`/`ActorLight`).
