# spells/drops
What: what a cloud lets go and what happens where it ends. A `ZoneDrop` flies under a pull of gravity (a frog, a drop of fire or of plague, a meteorite), rests on the ground or sticks to a body, and its children say the rest: `DropBlast` (damage, fire, stun, bursts), `DropPoison` (a contagious dose), `FrogHop` + `FrogCling` (a frog that jumps and sticks, ×0.6 pace each) and `FrogLook` (its frame).
Not: where and when a drop is born (the cloud's `ZoneDropper` and `DropLauncher`, in `zones/`), the cloud itself (`CloudLook`), the spell's numbers (they travel with the drop from the `SpellType`), the dents of Bombardment (061k).
Talks to: `DropLauncher` spawns the drop with `SpellSpawner` (a `projectiles` kind: the drop points at no spell); `ZoneDrop.landed/touched/ended` go up to its children; the server moves it and replicates `position` and `status`, every peer draws it; the blast and the poison reach everybody through `FxBus.broadcast` (the sounds, shakes and lights are the ones of each burst's name).
Edit: `rain_frog.tscn`, `fire_drop.tscn`, `plague_drop.tscn`, `meteor.tscn` (gravity, contact box, bursts, fire, stun, dose, sprite) · `data/fx/frog_*`, `fireball_*`, `plague_*`, `meteor_*`, `fire_drop_trail` · cues in `data/audio/fx_sounds.tres`. To add a drop: a scene with a `ZoneDrop` root, its effect children, a kind in `SpellSpawner.projectiles`, and a `DropLauncher` in a cloud.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_clouds.tscn` (F6; `wizard_god` picks the spell) · rules_suite `cloud_cases` (or `debug/suite/zone_run.tscn -- --only=cloud`) · `match_shots --cast=grfr|rifr|galp|likc|rtem --views=zone,foe_close`.
Not drawn: the scorch of the bolts and the meteorites (SCAR has no reader), the ash the exe puts in the air around Rain of Fire (the amount is NOT VERIFIED), the dynamic lights of the exe (the glow is additive sprites and `FxLightSpec`/`ActorLight`).
