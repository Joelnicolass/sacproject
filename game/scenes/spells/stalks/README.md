# spells/stalks
What: Meanstalks (`rter`, Persephone level 8). A `GroundZone` (`meanstalks.tscn`) with four `Stalk` children that grow out of roots in a ring of 9 to 21.6 m around the click while the wizard casts. Each `Stalk` looks (`StalkHunt`) for the nearest body of any side that is 10 to 45 m from its root and was not caught by this cast (`StalkRoster`), stretches its tip to it, hits it for 500, throws it up and lifts it to the top with the centre of the body on the tip (`StalkGrip`), throws it at 7 m from the top, and looks again; it sinks when the zone ends. The tube is drawn by `stalk.gdshader` from the eight control points `StalkLook` computes from the replicated `tip` and `growth`.
Not: casting (`ZoneAction` with `ring_count` 4 + `Caster`), the clock of the spell (`GroundZone`), the damage maths (`Health`, `SplashDamage.hurt`), the throw and the landing (`Tumbling`), the scar on the ground (no reader for that format).
Talks to: `ZoneAction.started` spawns the zone with `SpellSpawner` (kind `Meanstalks`, set in `match.tscn`) with the roots as `points`; the zone replicates `phase`, `cancelled` and every `StalkN:tip|growth|mode` (`synced`); a `Stalk` reads its zone and writes only its own tip, growth and mode; its `StalkHunt` asks `StalkRoster`; its `StalkGrip` calls `Tumbling`; `StalkLook` listens to `Stalk.mode_changed` for the sounds and the burst.
Edit: `meanstalks.tscn` (`end_s`, the four stalks) · `stalk.tscn` (`height_m`, `grow_s`, the `Hunt` knobs: `catch_m`, `release_m`, `wander_*`, `lift_*`; the light `Light` with the gizmo; the sounds) · `stalk.gdshader` material (`tip_mix`, `tip_from`, `uv_repeat`, `roughness_value`, `metallic_value`) · `data/catalog/spells/rter.tres` (damage 500, speed 30, acceleration 3, duration 60) · `data/fx/stalk_dirt.tres`, `stalk_grab.tres` and its light `data/fx/lights/stalk_grab.tres` · cues `stalk_rise|grab|fall` in `data/audio/fx_sounds.tres`. The tube mesh is baked by `tools/bake/make_stalk_tube.gd`. A stalk more = one more `Stalk` child with its `index`, the spell's `ring_count` and its three `synced` paths.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_stalks_tornado.tscn` (F6, `Targets.wizard_god` persephone, level 9) · `debug/suite/zone_run.tscn -- --only=stalk` or rules_suite `stalk_*` · `match_shots --god=persephone --level=8 --cast=rter --views=zone,foe_close` (prints each stalk).
Not drawn: the scar `mean` on the ground, the screen shake while it grows. NOT VERIFIED: which part of the tube the original paints with `dvin` and which with `vine` (the port blends from `dvin` to `vine` towards the tip, a knob).
