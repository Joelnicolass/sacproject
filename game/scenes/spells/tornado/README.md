# spells/tornado
What: Tornado (`nrot`, Stratos level 8). A `GroundZone` (`tornado.tscn`) whose `TornadoDrift` grows the funnel with the cast (to 0.8), then to 1, and wanders it around the click for 25 s (speed 6, acceleration 0.6, goals up to 50 m from home); `TornadoLift` pulls every creature and wizard on the ground that stands within 30 m of the funnel's wall toward a spiral around the axis and holds it up that tick, so Tumbling throws it (the fall is the only damage); `TornadoLook` lays the three parts of the funnel (upper wall `trn1`, lower wall `trn2`, ragged edge `edge`, one shader) on the 500 m axis bent by the four winds (`TornadoWinds`), raises dust (`tdst`) at the foot and carries a light.
Not: casting (`ZoneAction` + `Caster`), the clock (`GroundZone`), the throw and the landing (`Tumbling`), the funnel's mesh (a baked grid; the shader places it).
Talks to: `ZoneAction.started` spawns the zone with `SpellSpawner` (kind `Tornado`, set in `match.tscn`); the zone replicates `phase`, `cancelled`, its `position` and `Drift:size`; the winds come from `age_ticks` and a seed so nothing else is replicated; `Drift` is the only writer of the zone's position and of `size`; `Lift` reads both and calls `Tumbling`; `Look` reads them on every peer.
Edit: `tornado.tscn` (`end_s`, `Drift.cast_size|size_rate|arrive_m`, `Look.turn_s|edge_fps|dust_*`, the light `Look/Light` with the gizmo, the shader materials `energy` and `opacity`) · `data/catalog/spells/nrot.tres` (duration 25, speed 6, acceleration 0.6, effect range 50) · `data/fx/tornado_dust.tres`, `tornado_cast.tres` · cues `tornado_loop`, `tornado_cast` in `data/audio/fx_sounds.tres`. The two meshes are baked by `tools/bake/make_tornado_funnel.gd`.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_stalks_tornado.tscn` (F6, `Targets.wizard_god` stratos, level 9) · `debug/suite/zone_run.tscn -- --only=tornado` or rules_suite `tornado_*` · `match_shots --god=stratos --level=8 --cast=nrot --views=zone` (prints the size and speed).
NOT VERIFIED: the brightness the original gives the funnel (its energy 10 is a knob here: 3.2), the 500 m height of the column (sacengine's), the `tsfx` of its SPEL.
