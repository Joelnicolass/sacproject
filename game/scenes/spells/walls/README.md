# spells/walls
What: the five walls of the book (Firewall, Wall of Spikes, Wailing Wall, Vinewall, Fence). `SpellWall` is the wall: centred on the click, perpendicular to wizard -> click, both ends grow out to `effect_range_m` and stop at a building or the edge of the land, it rises, stands for `duration_s` and sinks (`WallRules`/`WallGround` hold the maths and the world questions). `WallTouch` is what it does to what is inside (damage with a ramp, stun, slow, mana drain, a state); `FencePosts` + `FenceZap` are the Fence; the rest of the children are the look (`WallLook` strip, `WallSpikes`, `WallVines`, `WallEmitter` sprites, `WallSpirit`, `WallSound`, `WallScars` + a `ScarMark` for the cracks of the Firewall and the Wall of Spikes, a `StateLight` that `WallLightFollow` keeps where it is seen).
Not: casting and cancelling (`WallAction` + `Caster`), the states a touch lays (`Stunned`, `PaceFactors`, `Vined`, `ManaPool`, `Health`), the bolt (`FxBus` + `BeamType`).
Talks to: `WallAction.started` asks `SpellSpawner` for a wall (kind `Firewall`, `WallOfSpikes`, `WailingWall`, `Vinewall`, `Fence`: the `zones` dictionary of the spawner in `match.tscn`, not preloaded, as the zones are), `finish` calls `arm()`, a cut calls `stop()`; the server grows the ends and replicates `age`, `left`, `right`, `vanish_at` (and the Fence's `posts`); every peer derives the height (`top()`) and draws; children read their wall (`@export var wall`), never name it by path.
Edit: each wall scene (the `Body` box = reach x height x thickness, with the gizmo; `grow_s`, `rise_s`, `vanish_s`; `WallTouch` knobs; the light colour) · `data/catalog/spells/<tag>.tres` (cost, damage, `effect_range_m`, `duration_s`) · `data/fx/wall_*.tres`, `data/fx/beams/wail_spirit.tres` (sprites) · `wall_*` keys of `data/audio/fx_sounds.tres` · the meshes are baked by `tools/bake/make_wall_meshes.py` (Blender CLI). Another wall = another `SpellWall` scene with other children.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_walls.tscn` (F6, `spell_N` then click the ground) · rules_suite `wall_cases` · `match_shots --cast=lwrf|laws|laww|lawv|cnef --cast-wait=6 --views=wall,overview`.
