# spells/wards
What: a body's shields and temporary forms. `Wards` (one per body) owns «what protects it now»; each `Ward` is one of them (LifeShield, SkinOfStone, AirShield, EtherealForm, Fireform, ProtectiveSwarm, HealingAura) with its factor, immunity and look; `BodyAura` is what the ward does to the bodies around (it burns, stings, pushes or heals: Healing Aura gives 60 a second to every creature of the carrier's side within 30 m, renewing a 256-tick heal in `HealEffect`); `WardEmitter` draws sprites around the body (while it is on, at its start or end, or while its spell is cast); `WardSound` plays its sounds; a `StateLight` child (`Glow`) lights the ground with the ward's colour while it is on (connected to `shown_changed` in the scene); `AuraImpact` marks the bodies the aura hurts.
Not: casting (`WardAction` + `Caster`), the transparency of Ethereal Form (`GhostForm`), the stone look (`Afflictions` owns `StoneLook`), who is shielded from Freeze/Petrify/Pull (they ask `Wards.shielded()`).
Talks to: `Health` asks `Wards.factor(kind)` and `immune()`; `Afflictions` asks `shielded()` and `stone_skin()` and listens to `Wards.changed`; `WardAction.finish` calls `Ward.start_for(spell)`; `Ward.shown` is replicated and every peer draws from `shown_changed`; the cast is read from the wizard's replicated cast particle (`Ward.cast_type`, one per ward; `WardAction.begin` hands its name to the Caster), so every peer gets `casting_changed`; `BodyAura.stung` (server) -> `AuraImpact` -> `FxBus.broadcast` (+ the `sound` of that FX's ParticleType).
Edit: each ward scene (factor, ranged_factor, look_scale, Aura reach radius with the gizmo, damage/push/heal knobs) · `data/fx/wards/*.tres` (sprites; the `*_cast` ones are the hands' particles and must live in `data/fx/`) · the `ward_*` cues of `data/audio/cues/` (sounds) · `data/catalog/spells/<tag>.tres` (cost, `duration_s`, `action`, `ai_use`). To add a ward: a scene + a child of `Wards` in `wizard_body.tscn` + its `:shown` in the synchronizer + a spell with `ward_action.tscn`.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_wards.tscn` (F6, key of the level-2 spell) · rules_suite `ward_cases` · `match_shots --cast=<tag> --views=wizard_close`.
