# spells/zones
What: a place on the ground with a clock. `GroundZone` goes through CASTING (the wizard still casts), ARMING (the cast ended, it waits), ACTIVE (it acts) and ENDING (it goes away), and its children do and draw what the spell does there: Explosion (5 fire balls, a blast 3.8 s after the cast starts that hurts, burns and throws), Frozen Ground (a snowball that flies 2 s, ice that grows and freezes, then breaks in 6 steps that hurt), Demonic Rift (a mouth that lets out up to 24 spirits).
Not: casting (`ZoneAction` in `actions/` + `Caster`), what a spell does to one body (`Frozen`, `Tumbling`, `Health`, `Burning`), Healing Aura (it is a creature's `Ward` in `wards/`), walls and clouds (061g, 061h).
Talks to: `ZoneAction.started` spawns the zone with `SpellSpawner` (the zone is a `SpellProjectile`), `finish` releases it and `cancel` cancels it; the server's `phase` is replicated and every peer draws from `phase_changed`; the `ZonePulse` children (`ZoneBlast`, `ZoneFreeze`, `ZoneShatter`) and `RiftSpawner` act on the server only; the blast and the ice break reach everybody through `FxBus.broadcast_scene`.
Edit: `explosion_zone.tscn`, `frozen_ground.tscn`, `demonic_rift.tscn` (timing knobs on the root, pulses, look; the numbers are the `SpellType` `.tres`) · `fire_sphere.gdshader`, `ice_ground.gdshader`, `rift_edge.gdshader`, `rift_trail.gdshader` · `data/fx/explosion_*.tres`, `snowball_*.tres`, `ice_shard.tres`, `rift_swirl.tres` · sounds: `explosion_*`, `frozen_ground_*`, `ice_shatter`, `rift_*` in `data/audio/fx_sounds.tres`. To add a zone: a scene with a `GroundZone` root, its pulses and look, a kind in `SpellSpawner.zones` (set in `match.tscn`; not a preload, to keep the SpellType, its action and the spawner from calling each other in a circle), and a spell with a `ZoneAction` scene.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_zones.tscn` (F6; `wizard_god` picks the spell) · rules_suite `zone_cases`, `rift_cases`, `aura_cases` (or `debug/suite/zone_run.tscn` alone) · `match_shots --cast=pxef|zorf|tfir|ruah --views=zone,foe_close` (prints each zone with its phase).
Not drawn: the scorch `scar` on the ground of Explosion (no reader for that format), the dynamic lights of the original (the glow comes from additive sprites).
