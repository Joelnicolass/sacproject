# spells/zones
What: a place on the ground with a clock. `GroundZone` goes through CASTING (the wizard still casts), ARMING (the cast ended, it waits), ACTIVE (it acts) and ENDING (it goes away), and its children do and draw what the spell does there: Explosion (5 fire balls, a blast 3.8 s after the cast starts that hurts, burns and throws), Frozen Ground (a snowball that flies 2 s, ice that grows and freezes, then breaks in 6 steps that hurt), Demonic Rift (a mouth that lets out up to 24 spirits), Erupt (the ground rises up to 15 m for 4.2 s, bursts, hurts, throws and sends a stunning wave; its `TerrainStamp` children lift the land and put it back).
Not: casting (`ZoneAction` in `actions/` + `Caster`), what a spell does to one body (`Frozen`, `Tumbling`, `Health`, `Burning`), Healing Aura (it is a creature's `Ward` in `wards/`), walls and clouds (061g, 061h).
Talks to: `ZoneAction.started` spawns the zone with `SpellSpawner` (the zone is a `SpellProjectile`), `finish` releases it and `cancel` cancels it; the server's `phase` is replicated and every peer draws from `phase_changed`; the `ZonePulse` children (`ZoneBlast`, `ZoneFreeze`, `ZoneShatter`) and `RiftSpawner` act on the server only; the blast and the ice break reach everybody through `FxBus.broadcast_scene`.

The sound of the burst (`erupt_burst`, the sample `erpt`) is NOT VERIFIED as the one of the original; the cast sounds of the build-up are not drawn. Volcano (`volcano_zone.tscn`, 061k-2): the `Mound` stamp is a picture (`assets/original/terrain/volcano_shape.png`, 33 x 33 from the original's volc.DATA) flattened to the height of the center that rises with the cast (4.44 s), falls to 25 % in the spell's 60 s and stays; the `Crater` stamp (25 m, 10 m deep) opens at the eruption and stays; `ZoneHeat` twice (the burn: 1000 a second to 20 m, nothing at 50 m, fire and a push; the slow push of 10 to 50 m), `ZoneBurst` twice (the eruption: 1000 splash to 50 m, ash, the `volc` mark; the ash every 1.5 s), a `ZoneDropper` (`BODY_OR_RING`) and a `DropLauncher` (`ZONE_COLUMN`, `shoots`) for the lava balls (`drops/lava_ball.tscn`), `ZoneStampStop` (a cut cast gives the ground back at once), `VolcanoLook` (the lava column and the splash: `volcano_lava/spatter.gdshader` over the meshes `volcano_*.res` baked by `tools/bake/bake_volcano_meshes.gd`; the smoke is the noisy layers of 061h, `CloudLook` + `cloud_sphere.gdshader`, under `CloudAt`; textures from `tools/import/import_terrain_stamps.py`), two `StateLight` of the lava's orange and the `FxLightSpec` of the eruption on `data/fx/volcano_ash_burst.tres`. Not verified: the sounds of the cast and of the lava loop (the eruption plays the Erupt sample), the strength (`energy`) of the three shaders under the filmic tonemap, how much a lava ball's rock weighs on screen. Bore (061k-3, `bore_zone.tscn`): the zone is born with the cast. `Drill` (`BoreDrill`: the rock, dirt and pebbles, an earth `StateLight`, the loop of the Soul Mole's bore sound and a `ScarMark` `bscr` at each step) walks a spiral from 5 m to the spell's reach at 0.9 of its speed; 10 s after the cast starts `Cutter` (`HoleCutter`, server only) walks the same spiral (`BoreSpiral`) at the full speed and asks the map's `Terrain` to take the ground away under it (`cut_hole`, which `HoleRules` allows or not), throws what is left without land (`Tumbling.catapult`), lets a `FallingChunk` drop and bursts earth with a light (`data/fx/bore_hole.tres`). At the end of the spiral it waits 3 s and 2 ticks and gives the holes back every 0.5 s (`fill_hole`), the last one first (`bore_repair`, with the sound `rkqX`); the zone is `open_ended` and finishes with the last one.
