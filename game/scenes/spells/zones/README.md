# spells/zones
What: a place on the ground with a clock. `GroundZone` goes through CASTING (the wizard still casts), ARMING (the cast ended, it waits), ACTIVE (it acts) and ENDING (it goes away), and its children do and draw what the spell does there: Explosion (5 fire balls, a blast 3.8 s after the cast starts that hurts, burns and throws), Frozen Ground (a snowball that flies 2 s, ice that grows and freezes, then breaks in 6 steps that hurt), Demonic Rift (a mouth that lets out up to 24 spirits), Erupt (the ground rises up to 15 m for 4.2 s, bursts, hurts, throws and sends a stunning wave; its `TerrainStamp` children lift the land and put it back).
Not: casting (`ZoneAction` in `actions/` + `Caster`), what a spell does to one body (`Frozen`, `Tumbling`, `Health`, `Burning`), Healing Aura (it is a creature's `Ward` in `wards/`), walls and clouds (061g, 061h).
Talks to: `ZoneAction.started` spawns the zone with `SpellSpawner` (the zone is a `SpellProjectile`), `finish` releases it and `cancel` cancels it; the server's `phase` is replicated and every peer draws from `phase_changed`; the `ZonePulse` children (`ZoneBlast`, `ZoneFreeze`, `ZoneShatter`) and `RiftSpawner` act on the server only; the blast and the ice break reach everybody through `FxBus.broadcast_scene`.
Edit: `explosion_zone.tscn`, `frozen_ground.tscn`, `demonic_rift.tscn` (timing knobs on the root, pulses, look; the numbers are the `SpellType` `.tres`) · `fire_sphere.gdshader`, `ice_ground.gdshader`, `rift_edge.gdshader`, `rift_trail.gdshader` · `data/fx/explosion_*.tres`, `snowball_*.tres`, `ice_shard.tres`, `rift_swirl.tres` · sounds: `explosion_*`, `frozen_ground_*`, `ice_shatter`, `rift_*` in `data/audio/fx_sounds.tres`. Erupt: `erupt_zone.tscn` has three `TerrainStamp` (Mound, Rebound, Wave: the radius and peak of each are its `Reach` cylinder, the shape its `StampProfile` curves), `ZoneErupt` (damage, throw, rocks and the light of the blast), `ZoneStunRing` (the wave that stuns) and `ZoneDust`; `synced` sends the stamps' clock to a client that joins late. The sound of the burst (`erupt_burst`, the sample `erpt`) is NOT VERIFIED as the one of the original; the cast sounds of the build-up are not drawn. To add a zone: a scene with a `GroundZone` root, its pulses and look, a kind in `SpellSpawner.zones` (set in `match.tscn`; not a preload, to keep the SpellType, its action and the spawner from calling each other in a circle), and a spell with a `ZoneAction` scene.
Check: `debug/spells/cast_terrain.tscn` (F6, Erupt with `james`) · `debug/spells/cast_zones.tscn` (F6; `wizard_god` picks the spell) · rules_suite `zone_cases`, `rift_cases`, `aura_cases`, `terrain_cases` (or `debug/suite/zone_run.tscn` alone) · `match_shots --cast=pxef|zorf|tfir|ruah|tpre --views=zone,foe_close` (prints each zone with its phase).
Not drawn: the scorch `scar` on the ground of Explosion and the marks of Erupt (no reader for that format yet, RFC-057 061k-5: a mark will be one more child of the zone that the burst plays, like `ZoneErupt.burst`), the dynamic lights of the original (the glow comes from additive sprites).
