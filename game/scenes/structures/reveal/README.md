# structures/reveal
What: how a cast tower is seen rising. `BuildReveal` (`build_reveal.tscn`): a line climbs from its base to its top with `CastTower.build_progress` (0 to 1); the tower is whole below it and dissolves with noise and a glowing edge above it; when it is done the tower's own materials come back. `BuildEdge` rides that line (`line_y()`): its `EdgeRings` throw flat blue rings while it rises (not in the last 0.5 s) and its `EdgeLight` grows with the progress, then fades out in 1 s when the tower stands. `BuildCues` plays the three moments on this peer: the start (a tower seen at the bottom), the end (seen rising, then standing) and the cut (it leaves still rising, the match going on: an explosion on the line).
Not: when or how fast the tower rises, nor whether it can be hit while it does (StructureAction, CastTower); it changes nothing of the match and does nothing in a headless process.
Talks to: reads `tower.build_progress` (replicated); BuildReveal swaps the surface materials of the MeshInstance3D under `visuals` for copies of `build_reveal.gdshader`; BuildEdge reads `reveal.line_y()` and drives `SpriteEmitter.set_active` and `ActorLight.set_level` / `set_lit`; BuildCues listens to `build_progress_changed` and `tree_exiting` and calls `FxBus.play` (local, never broadcast) at `BaseSpot`, saying `start_shown` / `done_shown` / `cut_shown`.
Edit: `build_reveal.tscn` (edge colour, energy, band width in metres, the noise and its scale, the glowing part); in `towers/tower.tscn`, `BuildEdge` (`energy_curve`, the ring window, `fade_out_s`) and its `EdgeLight` (colour, energy, range); the rings' size, rate and brightness in `data/fx/build_edge_ring.tres`; the moments in `BuildCues` (`start_fx`, `done_fx`, `cut_fx`: `data/fx/build_start.tres`, `build_done.tres` + `build_done_sparks.tres`, `build_cut.tres`, each with its light and sound).
Check: `debug/spells/manalith_build_area.tscn` (F6, cast the Manalith and watch it rise) · `debug/spells/manalith_build_cut.tscn` (F6, K in the middle of the rise cuts it) · flows `agent/flows/f388_build_area.json` (shots `f388_04` to `f388_08`) and `agent/flows/f389_build_feedback.json`.
