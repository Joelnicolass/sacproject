# units
What: every creature on the map: the `Unit` scene (`UnitBody` + Health, Afflictions, Orders, Targeting, one state per mode).
Not: wizards (`scenes/wizards/`), what the AI decides (`scenes/ai/`), how a spell is cast (`scenes/spells/`).
Talks to: `Health.died` goes up to `UnitBody`, which re-emits `died/mode_changed/struck` for match, souls and HUD; `SideVision.revealed` is heard by `TargetPicker`.
Talks to: `CorpseLook` listens to `Corpse` (`laid_down`, `dissolving_changed`) and lays the model down: `RagdollDriver` (physical skeleton baked by `tools/bake/bake_ragdolls.gd`, a direct child of `Unit`), else the death clip, else `TippingFall`; it fades it too. It only moves the model: nothing in the sim reads it.
Talks to: `CaptiveLook` listens to `UnitBody.mode_changed` and to `RagdollDriver.bound`, asks `CaptiveRules.on_change` what to show (hang as a rag doll, keep lying, stand up and appear, let go, or the clips rise/held/thrash) and does it with `RagdollDriver`, `TippingFall`, `BodyVisual.fade_to` and `ActorLight`. It is the only writer of `UnitBody.captive_role`; `Captive` only simulates (take, release, revive) and draws the ring. `CaptiveLook.hangs` is off: today every captive uses its clips.
Talks to: `UnitBody.type.body` (a `BodyFrame` scene in `bodies/`) is worn through `BodyMount`; its size and sockets reach the melee, the avoidance and `BodyCollider`, which is the only writer of the capsule. Its look (`bodies/<name>_look.tscn`: model, sounds, carried effect) is built only by a client.
Talks to: orders come down from `orders/` and `ai/` through `UnitOrders`; `UnitSpawner` builds each `Unit` and hands it its `CreatureType` (`UnitBody.type`) before it enters the tree; nothing below it looks a creature, ability or passive up by text.
Edit: `data/catalog/creatures/<nombre>.tres` (stats; drag its `ranged_attack`, `active_ability`, `on_damage` and `passives` from `data/catalog/abilities/`, `data/death_clouds/`, `data/passives/`) · `unit.tscn` (parts) · `states/` (one `UnitState` per mode) · `passives/`, `afflictions/`, `abilities/` (one child each).
Check: `debug/units/death_ragdoll.tscn` (ragdoll gallery) · `debug/units/death_ragdoll_wizards.tscn` (wizards) · `debug/units/*.tscn` (F6; e.g. `creature_cap.tscn`, `ability_spit.tscn`, `f118_stun.tscn`).
